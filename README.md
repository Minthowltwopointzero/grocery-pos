# Grocery POS System

A web-based Point of Sale (POS) system designed for grocery store management. This system helps manage products, inventory, sales transactions, and barcode scanning operations efficiently.

## About the Project

The Grocery POS System is developed to simplify daily store operations by providing product management, inventory monitoring, sales transactions, bulk product upload, and built-in barcode scanner functionality.

The system helps improve checkout speed, reduces manual product searching, and provides a more efficient way of managing grocery products.

## Features

### Product Management
- Add new products
- Update product information
- Delete products
- View product lists
- Manage product prices and details

### Barcode Scanner Integration
- Built-in barcode scanning functionality
- Quickly search and identify products using barcode
- Faster checkout process
- Reduces manual product selection errors

### Bulk Upload Products
- Upload multiple products at once
- Reduce manual product entry
- Validate product data before saving
- Faster product registration process

### Inventory Management
- Monitor available stocks
- Manage product quantities
- Keep inventory records updated

### Sales Management
- Process sales transactions
- Automatically retrieve product information through barcode scanning
- Record purchased products
- Track sales information
